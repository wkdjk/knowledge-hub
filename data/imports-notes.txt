Volumes are quarantine clearances recorded by Korea's Animal and Plant Quarantine Agency (QIA), converted to dried-equivalent tonnes (dried weight plus one third of frozen weight). Total imports peaked in 2018 and again in 2022 at around 238 tonnes, and have since eased to 172 tonnes in 2025 as the health-functional food market contracted. New Zealand's position swings more than any other supplier's — from 44% of the market in 2018 to around 23% in 2024–25 — because Korean buyers treat NZ product as the premium, discretionary end of the range. China has taken the largest share since 2024, supplying over half of all arrivals, while Russian volumes have drifted lower and Hong Kong's re-export trade has largely wound down. The 2026 chart bar is omitted until the year is complete; part-year figures appear in the summary above.
