2026 (Stats NZ Harmonised Trade exports, monthly — velvet dried/frozen/other, all destinations)
New Zealand's recorded velvet exports climbed from about 502 tonnes in 2019 to a record 992 tonnes in 2025, with a clear step up from 2022 onward. The dip to 680 tonnes in 2024 sits between two strong years and lines up with the same season Korean quarantine clearances of New Zealand velvet fell, pointing to a demand pause rather than a supply problem. Korea is only one of ten destinations in this series — it typically takes 60 to 140 tonnes a year, with China, Hong Kong and Taiwan taking the larger share — so the headline total reflects New Zealand's whole export book, not Korea alone. Destination-level detail, including the Korea-only figures, is in the downloadable monthly file. 2026 is omitted from the chart until the year is complete.
