2026 Q2 (real data — Stats NZ Harmonised Trade exports, dried/frozen/other velvet HS codes)
New Zealand's recorded velvet exports to Korea, China, Hong Kong and Taiwan rose from 574 tonnes in 2018 to a peak of 862 tonnes in 2023, dipped to 678 tonnes in 2024, then rebounded sharply to 991 tonnes in 2025 — the highest year in this series. This figure covers all four destinations combined, not New Zealand's total world exports, so it should be read as a directional trend rather than a full export total. The 2024 dip lines up with the same year QIA recorded a fall in Korean quarantine clearances of New Zealand velvet, while the 2025 rebound suggests renewed buying interest even as New Zealand's share of Korea's import market alone continued to soften. 2026 is omitted from the chart until the year is complete.
