Q2 2026 · reported 13 July 2026
Korean quarantine data to June 2026 shows a market that is still consolidating around Chinese dried velvet while New Zealand supply softens. In the twelve months to June, dried velvet arrivals from all origins rose 11% to 142.4 tonnes, driven almost entirely by China, whereas frozen arrivals — nearly all New Zealand product — fell by a third to 67.3 tonnes. New Zealand's combined arrivals were 31.5 tonnes in dried-equivalent terms, down 24% on the previous twelve months, leaving its share of the import market at around 23%. Domestic sentiment is mixed: Korea's self-sufficiency rate has recovered to 20% on lower imports, and the health-functional food segment recorded a third straight year of contraction, though the pace of decline is slowing.
